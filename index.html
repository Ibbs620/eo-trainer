What is this?

This is a tool for 3x3x3 Fewest Moves (FMC) competitors to train the Edge Orientation (EO) finding step of the Domino Reduction (DR) method. Typically, the first 8-10 minutes of an FMC attempt using DR are spent finding as many sequences that are 5 moves or less which solve EO.

How do I use it?

After clicking the "Start" button, a scramble and scramble image will be generated and the timer will start. In the below text box, enter as many EOs as you can find within the time limit. You can end the session early by clicking the "End" button.

To input an EO on the inverse scramble, or a combination of normal and inverse, surround the inverse portion of the EO with brackets, like F (U F), or (R U F). You can also invert the entire sequence by checking the "Inverse" box.
